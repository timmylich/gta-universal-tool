<p><b>TXD Tool and IMG Tool in one app</b> for mobile GTA San Andreas and SA-MP clients. Open a texture cache, swap a picture, jump into the game. No PC, no ads, no sign-up.</p>
<p><b>🎨 Textures</b></p>
<ul>
<li>gta3, gta_int, samp and other caches with previews, search and aliases</li>
<li>Replace or add from PNG/JPG, export to PNG, delete</li>
<li>DXT1, DXT5, RGBA8888, RGB565, RGBA4444, mipmaps, RLE</li>
<li>Size limit, including "As replaced", so the game stays smooth</li>
<li>alphamode, camnorm, streammode and any custom attributes</li>
<li>Rebuild the database to free space after many replacements</li>
</ul>
<p><b>❄️ Whole-map retextures</b></p>
<p>Winter mod, new roads, HD grass: add the folder with .txd files and the app does the rest. It knows where the game looks for every texture, handles different textures that share a name across TXDs, stores identical pictures once and skips what the mod did not change.</p>
<p><b>🚗 PC mods</b></p>
<p>Cars, skins and objects (dff + txd) move to mobile in a couple of taps. Several models at once; every texture gets a unique name, unused ones are dropped.</p>
<p><b>📦 IMG archives</b>: add, replace, export and delete .dff, .col and other files, rebuild the archive.</p>
<p><b>🔊 Audio</b>: listen to, export, replace and append tracks in .osw packs.</p>
<p><b>🛡 Safe</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Every game file is backed up before its first change</li>
<li>No ads, analytics or accounts: the app sends and collects nothing (it only checks GitHub for a new version)</li>
<li>1.4 MB, open source, free forever</li>
</ul>
<p>Step-by-step guide inside. Languages: English, Русский, Español, Bahasa Indonesia.</p>
