Mod mobile GTA San Andreas and SA-MP clients right on your phone. Open a texture cache, replace a picture, start the game. No PC, no ads, no sign-up.

🎨 TEXTURES
• gta3, gta_int, samp and other caches — with previews, search and aliases
• Replace and add from PNG/JPG, export to PNG, delete
• DXT1, DXT5, RGBA8888, RGB565, RGBA4444, mipmaps, RLE
• Size limit, including "as replaced", so the game stays fast
• alphamode, camnorm, streammode and any custom attributes
• Rebuilding a database frees space after many replacements

📦 IMG ARCHIVES
• Add, replace, export and delete .dff, .col and other files
• Rebuild the archive

🔊 AUDIO
• Listen to, export, replace and add tracks in .osw packs

🚗 PC MODS
• Cars, skins and objects (dff + txd) in a few taps, several models at once; every texture gets a unique name
• Retextures: textures from a mod's .txd go where the game looks for them; add a whole folder at once

🛡 SAFE
• Every game file is backed up before its first change; restore the original with one tap in Settings
• No ads, analytics or accounts; the app has no internet access
• 1.4 MB, open source, free forever
